# LEOGO DIGITAL MARKET — Proposed Return, Refund & Exchange Policy
**Status: DRAFT — NOT YET CUSTOMER-FACING. Do not submit as an active Merchant Center policy.**
**Prepared:** 17 November 2026
**Applies to:** Eligible physical product orders fulfilled to customers within Kenya. Services, accommodation, rent instalments, financial products, and other distinct workflows require separate terms.

## Customer-friendly proposed terms — awaiting owner approval

### Returns and exchanges
Customers may request a return or exchange for an incorrect, damaged, defective, missing or materially misdescribed item. They may also request a change-of-mind return for eligible non-defective products. **Proposed standard return-request period: 7 calendar days after confirmed delivery. OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED.** Mandatory rights under applicable Kenyan consumer law remain unaffected.

For a change-of-mind return, items should be unused, complete and returned with original accessories and packaging where reasonably possible. Items with hygiene, perishability, safety, customisation or other lawful restrictions may have category-specific return exceptions clearly disclosed before purchase. A defect claim must not automatically be denied because packaging has been opened or because a voluntary return period has expired.

### How to request a return
Sign in to LEOGO Digital Market, open the relevant order and use the after-sales support/return or refund request feature. Select the order and item, explain what went wrong, attach available evidence, and choose a preferred resolution. If unable to access the account, contact LEOGO support by emailing **leogodigitalmarket@gmail.com** or calling **0700 192 545**, with the order reference. Do not send original identity documents by open email.

LEOGO coordinates seller review and can intervene in unresolved cases. Where an item must be returned, LEOGO provides an approved pickup-station or return-shipment instruction after the return is accepted. Do not send goods to an unverified address.

### Returns shipping and charges — PROPOSED
For verified wrong, damaged, defective or materially misdescribed items, LEOGO/seller arranges the appropriate return handling without charging the customer for correcting the seller-side problem, subject to applicable law. For an approved change-of-mind return, the customer may bear the disclosed return-shipping cost. No undisclosed restocking fee should be charged. **OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED** for these financial responsibilities and any exceptions.

### Assessment and refunds
After reviewing the evidence and, where necessary, inspecting the returned item, LEOGO coordinates a replacement, exchange, repair, refund or other lawful remedy as appropriate. Approved refunds should normally go back to the original payment channel, unless the customer expressly accepts another lawful option. **Proposed processing target: within 7 business days after approval and successful receipt/inspection where needed, excluding external payment-provider delays. OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED.** This is not a representation of the currently implemented payout SLA.

Keep customers informed of their case and provide an escalation route through LEOGO customer support. Statutory cancellation and remedies take precedence over any narrower voluntary return rule.

### Exceptions and separate plans
Perishables, opened hygienic products, made-to-order goods, digital services and other special categories require explicit category-specific rules where legally permissible. Lipa Pole Pole, rent instalments, accommodation and service quotations are not governed by this general physical-product return policy; their applicable terms must be shown separately.
